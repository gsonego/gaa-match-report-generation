# Ethics

- Data: public match data from Wikipedia (All-Ireland Senior Football Championship pages, 2021-2024), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Page revisions are fixed by revision id (`data/raw/*.meta.json`, summarised in `data/README.md`).
- No human participants: the ground truth is labelled by the author. No personal data beyond the names of players and teams as published on the match pages.
- Procedure: no DkIT ethics application form is required for this design. Confirmed by email by Dr Jack Mc Donnell of the Department of Computing Science and Mathematics on 21 October 2026 and recorded in [`meetings.md`](meetings.md). The email is attached to the interim report (Appendix A).
- If external raters or published match reports are added later, they need separate approval or terms checked first (supervisor question 4, still open).
